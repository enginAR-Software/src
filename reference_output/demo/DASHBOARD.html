FieldShift goal dashboard

Using a range of data, estimate the most suitable region that North American wheat will migrate to under climate change; train a mathematical model that computes the economically most efficient crop rotation for the regions split into parcels along that path; and make farming easier by telling farmers each year when to plant and which crop.

Planting windows from the NASA NEX-GDDP (SSP2-4.5) daily projection with sourced thresholds: median of 2016–2024 and 2041–2049. Winter wheat is sown in the previous autumn.
